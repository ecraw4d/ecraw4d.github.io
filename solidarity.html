Solidarity newspaper archive

Solidarity was the weekly newspaper of the Industrial Workers of the World (IWW) from 1909 to 1917. This archive makes the full text of every surviving issue keyword-searchable. Search it below, or browse by year.

About this archive

Page scans come from the Mapping American Social Movements Project at the University of Washington. I ran them through Google Document AI's optical character recognition (OCR) to make the text searchable. The text is uncorrected machine output, so expect misspellings and columns that run together. Always check a passage against the scan before quoting it.

The campfire drawing is traced from a scan of page 157 of Rebel Voices: An I.W.W. Anthology (1964), edited by Joyce L. Kornbluh.
