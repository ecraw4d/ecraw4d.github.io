CLEVELAND PACKING HOUSE WORKERS STRIKE The strike of the packing house workers in Cleveland is now entering the second week and sees the ranks unbroken. As usüal when the working class ask for more bread the hirelings of the capitalist class hearken to the call of their master, and on the 2nd of May one of the strikers was shot by an imported thug. Peaceful picketing is a thing of the past. Pickets are arrested for walking past the packing houses, and the capitalist dailies are publishing false and misleading statements thinking that will cause a stampede back to the packing houses. But the strikers are wise to their tricks. With renewed vigor the strikers are going to use new methods and tactics to beat the boss; by changing their tactics it will keep the boss guessing.
