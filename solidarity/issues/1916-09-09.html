The all-absorbing feature of the Minnesota strike, just now are the indictments against Carlo Tresca, Joe Schmidt and Sam Scarlett, together with Philip Masonovitch, Mrs. Masonovitch, Philip Orlandich, Joe Cirsegarievich and Joe Nicich. These indictments meant to intimidate the strikers and break the strike, have failed of their object. John Pancner and Charles Jacobson of the Virginia branch are on the Vermilion Range. Not only have the indictmen failed to break the Minnesota strike, but they have given great impetus to the general strike movement in behalf of the indicted men and women. The general strike movement has been advocated all along by the Italian I. W. W. press, Tresca's L'Avvennire, and sympathetic papers.
