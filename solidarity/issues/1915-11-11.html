HAVE AVE YOU ever given thought to the great part that advertising has played in modern industrial development--the evidences of which we see at every turn in a myriad of different forms? It is a condemnation of the system that an article of inferior merit may be made to enjoy a larger sale over a much better competing article solely through a well directed naiton wide advertising campaign. Today, second only to having an article of unquestioned worth, is the necessity for having the means of making its merits known on a vast scale. An interesting instance is that of a firm that some ten years ago were making a good grade of roll roofing, but like other ventures of that period were producing but a small fraction of the peoples needs for roofing. An advertising campaign was then started to aquaint possible buyers with the merits of this style roofing, and to creat a consumers demand for their particular brand. campaign has developed until today their advertisements reach twenty eight million families fifty times each year. As a result this company has commanded and continues to hold the dominating position in this particular line of manufacture.
