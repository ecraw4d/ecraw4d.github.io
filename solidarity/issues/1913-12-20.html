It is lunacy, and worse, to hold, as Sam Gompers does, to craft unionism in spite of the development of consolidated capitalism and industry. It is good, sound sense, to recognize that, industries are centering in fewer hands and that labor must or ganize industrially and as class in accordance with such dency. And it is such sense that the I. W. W. displays. It is lunacy, and worse, to hold as Sam Gompers does, that the working class can gain power by boring from within capitalist political parties, and getting a cabinet member in the Federal administration, while that same administration prosecutes the fellow unionists of that cabinet member in Northern Colorado. It is good, sound sense to hold that real political power is based on economic control and that such control exercised by the working class, as it is exercised by the ultra capitalists, in industry, will save labor from persecution and decline. And it is such sense that the I. W. W. displays. It is lunacy, and worse, to hold as Sam Gompers does, that the present capitalist wage system is just and permanent and will endure despite evolutionary tendencies and revolutionary movements. It is good, sound sense, to know that economic science shows the present system to be one of transition, tending to the development of forces (among them an industrially organized working class (aiming at its overthrow. And it is such sense, that the I. W. W. displays. For Sam Gompers' benefit we quote, in conclusion, Roger Babson, eminent Wall Street authority on economic movements. Says Babson, regarding the A.
