THE ONLY FIGHT WORTH FIGHTING It is not because of sentimental reasons that the I. W. W. is opposed to war but simply because capitalistic war offers us no goal worth fighting for. Freedom of the press, of speech and assembly have become empty catch words. They are now degenerated inte mere "favors" granted or revoked at the whim of some two-by-twice police lieutenant or petty official or other. As far as "democracy" is concerned, we have found that the only democracy that means anything to us is Industrial Democracy, and that this is only obtainable by action on the job, and not through governmental means of any kind. Not cause war is replete with horror do we oppose it; we would not count the price if the benefits were commensurate. But the poor duped workers foot the bills and the parasites get all the gains. War is redolent with rotting flesh, mangled limbs, tangled guts and steaming pools of human blood; sickening with cries, groans and screams of agony, re- 0234 volting with white corpses, grey cripples, nerve-wrecks and syphilitics, piteous with the pain of mothers, sweethearts, wives and children-but all this does not damn war so deeply and everlastingly as the fact tha it is incomprehensibly idiotic and unnecessary. And if slaves are stupid enough to want to mix up in a master class quarrel over commercial or territorial supremacy, war debts or what not, then they should be willing to pay the price without a whimper. And if mothers, wives and sweethearts permit their loved ones to go abroad to fight someone else's battles, instead of advising them to stay home and fight their own, they deserve but scant pity, for they are merely paying the price of their Entered as second clasmatter March the office at Chicago, Illinois, under the act of Tom Tracy and fifty-eight of the survivors of the Everett massacre are "free"; but what the hell were they ever pinched for, anyhow?
