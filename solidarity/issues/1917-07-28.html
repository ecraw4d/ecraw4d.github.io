The master class, besten at their own of law and order, lost no time in using other methods which suited them better. The mines of Bisbee were practically shut down; mine owners of Wall street were losing thousands and thousands of dollars daily and hourly. Parts of the mines were caving in, but rather than, grant the workers their modest and just demands, they resorted to mob rule. The happenings of the last few days have been of great educational value not only to the 4,500 striking miners of Bisbee, but to slaves all over the world. Small business men, inflamed by the local kept press and the corpora tion's howls, and of course as they considered their economic interest to be with the masters instead of with the advancing proletariat, they lined up against the miners. THE IMPENDING CHANGE Even casual observers are becoming aware of the fact that we are living in desperate times and that some great social readjust ment or upheaval is imminent. Nothing like the present period of acute industrial discontent has ever been known before. Never in our time have the rumblings of world wide industrial discontent been so pronounced or so significant. What the outcome will be it is, of course, impossible to predict; but as far as the I. W. W. is concerned, it is hoped that the culmination will not be a blind and bloody outburst of mere mob violence. There is nothing the master class would like better than to bring things to a sudden head and then make abortive any outcome that would benefit the workers. The One Big Union idea is to build up a powerful organization in the industries where bullets and bayonets are futile and where the menace of machine guns can have no effect. That a great change of some sort is inevitable almost any member of the master class will 'admit as an individual, but as a class they are fanatically determined to retain their soft seats upon the backs of the producing proletariat as long as possible. This is the position of the tyrants of all times, and they would not be true to their class interests and instincts if they acted otherwise. But it is unquestionably true that the Big Money Interests of America are reeling along the perilous path that leads to revolution. Blind with greed and puffed up with power, the Parasites of Industry are reveling in debauchery and persecution as did the ruling as before they were brushed into oblivion with the broom of history.
