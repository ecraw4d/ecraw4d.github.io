W. and Line Up for Better Conditions (Special to Solidarity) Kansas City, June 12. Rain has again delayed the harvest, and from all reports the Kansas harvest will start about June 25th. Kansas is flooded with men; almost every town is trying to chase the workers out of town, as the advertisements in big eastern dailies bring workers here by the thousands and overflood the labor market. All reports are to the effect that the I. W. W. will get a large membership this summer. The I. W. W. men are showing the stiffs how to eat, while the unorganized are going hungry or eat the leavings of the bunch. Farmers are thinking of paying $2.50 a day now in several places. Caldwell chased 500 men out of town on a freight train a few days ago.
