Crayon Portraits Of Joe Hill For some time the Publishing Bureau has been receiving requests to furnish readers of Solidarity throughout the country with portraits of Joe Hill. We felt some hesitation in making these up due both to the possibility of the motive being ascribed to a commercialization of Hill's fate, and also on account of the uncertainty as to being able to cover the expense. These requests became so numerous and insistent, that it was decided last week to do a very limited number of them as a special service for these readers. The portraits will be from the charcoal drawing by Chumley that appears in this issue of Solidarity, size 11x14 inches, on an artistically toned heavy sheet with suitable inscription in unobtruding type. This sheet will be pasted to a heavy double thick slightly larger sheet to act as a hanger with black and red ribbon bow. This arrangement will make the portrait suitable either for hanging as it is, or to be framed as desired. No adequate description of the finished appearance can be given-suffice to say that no attempt has been made to produce anything cheap, as we realize that those wanting portraits will want the best obtainable both as to appearance and durability. The prices quoted below will barely if actually cover the cost of making and delivering them. Now understand stock and material has been bought for only a bare hundred or so, and unless more orders than this are received at once, this is all that will be made up. If you want one of these portraits, the order must be sent in at once with the amount named to cover cost. Portraits will be mailed in about two weeks, depending on circumstances. The cost of single portrait suitable for framing or hanging as it is, will be twenty five cents postpaid. If you wish we can save you the trouble of having the framing done.
