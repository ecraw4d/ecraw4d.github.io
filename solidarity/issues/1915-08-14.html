NILSSON Those who agitate for greater and more efficient military power seek to persuade us that this is the only way in which WE can protect OUR COUNTRY and OUR LIBERTY. They tell us in so many words that "there always has been wars, and there always will be wars, as long as human nature remains unchanged." It is true that there have been wars as far back as history can tell us of, and it is horribly true that there are wars today. It only remains to be seen if wars are due to the same causes now as in the past, and if we have the same interest in warfare as our ancestors had. What was the primary cause of war between Indian tribes? And who gained by a victory? Then there were wars between nations because it was easier to steal a living abroad, than to produce a living at home. Those who fought served their own interests by fighting. There is war now because a homeless, outcast class of roen have been told, and believe, that they have a home and a country to fight for.
