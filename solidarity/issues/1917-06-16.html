Well, the next thing will be something else. The flag may possibly make a good mask for militarism, but it makes a damn poor excuse for scabbery. Industrial democracy is the only democracy that means any to the workers. The thar industrial democracy is the ONLY war worth fighting-and you don't have to go to France to fight for it, either. The war of the parasites is for the freedom to exploit; the war of the workers is for freedom from exploitation. What are YOU going to fight for? Long hours, short pay, the speed up system, and the rotten sys that is built upon them are the WORST enemies of the worktem that is a charity, should begin at home. Join the I. W. W. Arm yourself with credentials, literature, and wooden shoes. Get into the first line trenches of industry and DO YOUR BIT. Unionism implies the absence of division. It means uniting as a unit against the enemy. Craft separation and class solidarity are irreconcilable opposites; the one makes the other absolutely impossible. Unionism means unity.or it means nothing at all. A. F. of L. has functioned and is functioning not to overthrow capitalism, but to sustain it. If you are out for the whole works and want to belong to a 100 per cent unión organization, join the I. W. W. If you are not, stay where you are; we don't need you. a little craft union is a good thing for a few workers, a BIG industrial union would be a better thing for all workers. If the little strike, made possible by the craft form of organization, enables you to win little demands, a big strike, made possible by the One Big Union form of organization, would enable you to get anything you ask for. You can have POWER only to the extent that you are ORGANIZED. Organize in the I. W. W. to acquire the greatest power for the purpose of winning the greatest possible prize the world for the workers. Can you imagine any greater absurdity than that of working men organizing for the purpose of merely continuing their existence under Capitalism; organizing to merely gain a few pennies more, or a few moments less per day; when they might just as well use their incomparable power in industry to put an end to the system of parasitism altogether? If you want to organize for Capitalism, war and exploitation, join the A. F. of L.; if you want to organize for the working class, peace and emancipation, join the I. W. W. The Oligarchs of Industry seem not only determined to make. us do what they want us to, but to make us like it as well. We're learning, and they can have no kick coming when we get ready to pay them back in full-just as we learned from them.
