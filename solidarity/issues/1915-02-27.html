Some members mistake the the traveling spirit of some western workers for a revolutionary spirit. This is very mis leading, as it fosters the idea that all you have to do to better conditions is to quit your job and travel 500 or 1,000 miles to another job that is as bad or worse. Others make the mistake of keeping their local in the "jungles" and holding out for higher wages, while the "scissorbill" is doing the work and getting the pay. If we want results in the harvest fields this coming summer, we will have to devise ways and means of keeping members in a certain locality in touch with one another, and also keep the different localities in touch with the doings in other localities. To properly handle the situation we will have to arrange to have a secretary at some central point in the harvest fields and also have temporary secretaries at points of advantage.
