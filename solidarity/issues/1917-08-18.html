"Happened" is not the best word, perhaps -we should say "engineered." Many men have been killed, and in a great many cases no one has been apprehended. This condition has naturally led to a peculiar state of mind in the people of this district; they have seen so many crimes commit ted; so many men die lingeringly with miner's consumption, and so many queer things in general, that nothing less than a most particularly shocking event ever stirs more than passing interest. The public was dumbfounded at the Speculator mine disaster, people stood Adast yet in the short time that has elapsed since that terrible catas trophe they have almost dismissed it from their minds; that is, most of those who lost no friends or relatives have become less keenly resentful. This is due in part to the war, registration, conscription, etc. But in all the lurid history of Butte no event ever occurred 'so starting, so savage, of such sinister, evil portent as the inconceivably vicious murder of Frank Little, a cripple, last Wednesday morning. Every decent-thinking man, woman and child in this community was stricken with horror when the news of this frightful crime was published. Not one could comprehend it, many refused to believe it at first, but finally came the moment when we were forced to admit that here in Butte, our own home town, a crime more terrible than any atrocity of which the belligerents of Europe, or even the "Unspeakable Turk" of our cfilidhood recollections, have ever been accusód. An Appeal to All Workers in the Railroad Transportation Industry Fellow Workers, this is the golden opportunity for which you have been looking . Don't skip the body of this article and glance through it for a catch, for there is none. Read it over and reason it out for yourself, and see whether or not it is the Golden Opportunity claimed and whether it is of interest to you. No doubt you are well aware of the Importance of the railroad transportation industry. Suffice to say that it is of prime importance to produc tion of all kinds, and very necessary as a means of distribution of commodities such as foodstuffs, clothing, raw materials on their way to centers of production, finished articles on their way to polats of consumption, as passenger carriers, and various other functions which make the industry so vital to the welfare and comfort of the people. The present inhuman war and slaughterfest in Europe has stimulated this industry to bounds unprecedented in history. Prosperity abounds in all industries as never before. Industries which before the war had operated at normal are now operating double shifts and reaping an undreamed of harvest of wealth. The railroad transportation industry most of all has profited by this abnormal condition. It can no longer meet the demand that has been made upon it for rolling stock. The railroad companies are making frenzied efforts to cope with this situation and are thriving as never before. It has been said that the railroads are the pulse of the nation. If this be true then it is at fever heat that this pulse is now throbbing. Prosperity for Whom? You will admit that old Dame Fortune has spread with a lavish hand prosperity upon all the industries in the land. Yes, but to whom has she been so generous? Who profits by this cruel war? Do you raliroad workers? If so, in what way? No, you do not. On the contrary, you are making it possible for those who own all the industries and railroads to more than double their profits through the extra demand created by the war. The members of the Capitalist Class alone are prospering. First let us hesitate a minute and disclose the true source of all wealth. Wealth is simply stored up labor power. It represents that part of the product of the worker that was produced for profit alone, not for use. On the average, one-fifth of what the worker produces, or its equivalent in makers, etc, will be needed. All of these opera of the workers were working hand in hand, as it were, REGARDLESS OF RACE, CREED OR COLOR! This harmonious operation is the result of ORGANIZATION, granting the fact that rallroad transportation is an indus try in itself then you have it, INDUSTRIAL ORGANIZATION. That proves you are organized industrially for the profit of the boss: You must organize yourselves industrially for yourselves just as the boss has you organised for himself. All of you are mere cogs in the machinery of railroad trans portation and as individuals or small groups you are not so important but that you can be replaced, but, as a whole, you are of extreme importance and cannot be readily replaced, if at all. Some of the readers of this article are possibly organized at present in craft unions or brotherhoods. Let me show you that the craft form of organization is no longer able to function in the interests of the workers, that it is no longer alive and progressive, that it is now unable to fulfill the mission for which it was intended. It was useful in its day just as the ox cart before the coming of the automobile, but because it was useful once does not say that it will continue so indefinitely. In fact, it was out of date even at its inception, and now, after the employers have perfected their organizations and associations the craft unions of the workers cannot cope successfully with them.
