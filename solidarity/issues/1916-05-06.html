W'S EVERYWHERE The new Domestic Workers' Industrial Union in Denver is growing rapidly and the outlook for a fighting organization of several thousand members is good. But funds for securing a clubhouse, or house for the girls when out of employment, are badly needed and at once. Owing to the conditions peculiar to this industry, it is nécessary for the workers to line up the places of employment by tactics on the job. When one is discharged for such tactics, another is sent on to take her place, thus wearing down the resistance of the employer. The home is badly needed for the girls thrown out this way, as a refuge until they are placed on another job, there to repeat the good work.
