John R. Lawson, the mine workers' leader in Colorado, and of the frame-up against Mooney in San Francisco is the acquittal by a jury in Everett. Wash., of Thomas H. Tracy, one of the I, W. W.'s indicted on a scumped-up charge of murder, Indictment and trial resulted from an attempted visit to Everett, several months ago, of a group of I. W. W.'s. However, unwelcome to certain citizens of Everett their visit may have been, they had a right to enter the city. However false the doctrine they wished to spread may be, they were entitled to the chance to present it. The right to preach truth is in danger whenever the right to preach error is denied. But Everett's Commercial Club felt otherwise, The I. W. W.s were coming to say things to the laboring people, which might make them discontented. It mattered little to this club whether the prospective talks were true or false. There might be good grounds for discontent among the city's laboring class, but if so, it wanted knowledge thereof withheld. So the sheriff of the county was induced to make a lawless attack on the visitors as they were about to land from a steamer at the dock. Some were shot, others beaten and arrested, and many were driven away. In the course of this disorder 023% a deputy sheriff was killed. No indictments were presented against the sheriff, his assistants or members of the Commercial Club for their part in the riot. But the L. W. W.'s were prosecuted for defending themselves. It is to be hoped that the failure of the prosecution will discourage further attempts to suppress discontent by force, not only in Everett but elsewhere. Industrial organization is the Royal Road to Emancipation! The only function political government can perform is to safeguard the interests of the ruling class. "Not the wish of the work ers but the will of Wall Street" is the slogan in Washington. When not a turn-coat or a traitor, his sincere stupidity is often more harmful than his malice. The politician is just as useless as the parasite-and twice as dangerous. THE WORKERS' WAR IS FOR EMANCIPATION Because the capitalist class of America saw fit to plunge the nation into one of the most hopeless and idiotic wars in history is no reason why the workers should neglect to "hold all they've got and get all they can." The slaves had a war on their hands before the Oligarchs started theirs and the workers' war is of far more importance. And this greatest and holiest of struggles will continue during the "war" and after it is over "until the workers organize as a class, take possession of the earth and the machinery of production, and abolish the wage system." The revolutionary workers of Russia have started out to do this very thing and that is why the Russian revolution has become the nightmare of industrial as well as political tyranny the world over. they have no control. The law of supply and demand at the mercy of which the parasites are, not only makes it impossible for them to pay for commodities (such as steel, lumber, coal, labor power, etc.) the price that they feel like paying, but also prevents them from selling their commodities for any price that they feel like. Here are the workers in the steel industry. Through the power of One Big Union they effect an increase in their wages. This, however, does not affect the price of steel, however much the owners of that industry might feel like raising the cost of steel. Those owners have got to compete with owners of other steel industries in other countries, which keeps the price of the commodity steel where it is. But supposing the steel industry has a monopoly in the country; supposing it is a trust. Even then there is competi tion on account of other steel trusts in other countries shipping steel in that particular country. This same capitalist competition or this same law of supply and demand, holds good for those manufacturers that produce the necessities of life that the workers consume. True at first increased wages will create a bigger demand for all such commodities and so bring about a scarcity which will raise prices, but pretty soon through other capitalists starting to produce the same commodities or starting to produce those com modities in bigger quantities the price will again fall to where it was before and may be lower. Then also which way will it affect the workers when the price of steel goes up or the price of automobiles or of silk gowns, diamond dog collars or Havana cigars? So every way we look at it an increase in wages benefits the proletariat and is another step in making the vampire class gorge up the blood that they have sucked out of the workers, their wives and children. The L. W. W. is the most revolutionary organization in so far as it not only clearly recognizes this fact but also takes its measures accordingly.
