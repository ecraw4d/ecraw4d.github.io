Mattingly On the economic field, the war of the sexes tends to become submerged in the war of the classes. Woman is no longer "the slave of a slave," as formerly. She is becoming more and more the victim of direct action in exploitation the slave of the capitalist. Woman's "economic independence" will be a farce, when patterned after that of man's. There can be no independence for either sex, so long as a few own and control the land and machinery necessary for the existence of all. She who would be free must not only herself strike the blow, but who knould how to strikest notre important than birth control is economic control. And more important than sexemancipation is class liberation. To ignore economics is to enslave the living. To free one sex is to demoralize the whole race. Solidarity has no apologies to offer for this, its Woman's Special Edition. The latter speaks for itself in a manner creditable to all of its contributors, without whose hearty co-operation it would have been impossible. The woman worker is well represented, from the intellectual teacher to the factory girl and the floor washer. Woman is present, not only in her own home, but in the home of other women, too; a fact that to woman, is more tragic than appears from the mere statement.
