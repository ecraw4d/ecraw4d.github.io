Lambert, Richard Brazier and William Wiertels Botered on anond class matter March ost office at Chicago, An ounce of sabotage is worth a ton of legislation. The organization of the workers into One Big Union is not an idea hatched from the brain of any one thinker. It is a step in the evolution of society just as necessary and as inevitable as the forma tion of the first social units the gens, the phratery, the clan and the tribe. It is the outgrowth of the instinct to survive on the part of the wage-worker surrounded by the hostile environment of present day industrialism. It is the answer of the collective intelligence of the only virile portion of society the producing class, to the social readjustments required by be machine process. It is the shadow that modern industry casts upon the future. Sabotage is a weapon of industrial warfare, it is one form of strking; and, like any other method of striking, is primarily intended to "hit the boss in the pocketbook." Sabotage is just as necessary to the wage-slave of today as were the bow and arrow to his prehis toric forebear. In seeking to "outlaw" sabotage, the legislators of the Western states are attempting to place human necessity outside of the law. But "necessity knows no law." They can just about as easily stop sabotage with legislation as a medicine-man could stop a tornado with a tom-tom. Sammy Gompers pledged" the American labor movement to Capitalism for the term of the war; but whonell pledged the American labor movement to Sammy? The Everett and San Francisco cases evidence an attempt of Plutocracy to go the limit in wiping Labor Unionism from the map. The "anti L. W. W. laws" indicate a desire on the part of several State governments to disarm the worker completely and turn him over, helpless, to the mercy of his enemies, the restriction of the right of free speech, free press and free assembly that is going on under the poison gas cloud of adventitious "patriotism," seeks to gag the worker, in the face of crying injustice. All of which is just as it should be according to the parasites, who desire nothing better than that the exploitation, jailing, shooting and hanging of union men can continue indefinitely without comment or protest. If union men only TALKED and DREAMED they would be latighed to scorn and no one would fear them; but let them ACT, and they are feared and execrated. Right away they are "criminals" and "outlaws." And that is why the predatory class in America hates the I. W. W. more virulently than anything else. It is because we have the clear vision to see the naked cruelty of exploitation, the alert brains to understand the modern industrial process, the good indgment to apply the tactics that tell, and the strong hands to reach out for what we want and take it-that is why they hate us. We are persecuted and lied about more than others because we are feared more; more than the philosopher with his idle definitions, more than the political trickster with his meaningless ballot, more than the craft "unionist" with his stupid contracts more than anything on earth are we hated by the parasites and their underlings. And all because they realize that we are aware of the historic mission of pour clado away with theirs, because we represent the dynamic force in modern society the young and vigorous element in an epoch "rotten-ripe for change the germination of the New Order in the blood-soaked soil of the old.
