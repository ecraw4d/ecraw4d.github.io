Mattingly "Conspiracy" Still In The Ring With the evidence now at hand the Mesaba Range cases appear to have resolvéd themselves under the cunning hand of the prosecution as follows: All murder charges have been dropped; Viewing the situation from another angle, that of the actual contact of the soldiers in the field, we cannot but see how this too, fends to weaken the spirit of nationalism. Soldiers of a dozen different nations fighting side by side, co-operating in a common offense or defense, must eventually learn to respect one another. That respect will be extended to the enemy as well. Add to this the intellectual stimüfus of travel and association in new environments, and its ultimate effects. upon return soldiers must be little short of revolutionary. But what of the labor movement? We can see no reason to doubt that, with the close of military struggle, the broken strands of the European labor organizations will be brought together once more, and woven more securely in an international sense. The associations of the war will have prepared the ground, and the enormous stimulus to the machine process during the struggle will supply an unbreakable fibre for the New International. Altogether we feel safe in venturing the prediction that Europe and its labor movement will emerge from the maelstrom of war, with its feet on the ground and its face towards the rising sun of industrial freedom. Capitalism is doomed, war or no war!
