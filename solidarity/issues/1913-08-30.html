Whatever may be said for or against the diverse THEO- RIES of the revolutionary union movement, one thing stands out SOLIDARITY worthy of notice. It reads: "New York, Philadelphia, and Boston are lined up solidly with the 'decentralizers' as they are called to make the 1. W. W. 'come clean,' and while there has not been any considerable noise among the rank and file, their voice will be heard in the convention; the proper place to settle this vital question which is at present agitating the entire organization; provided, of course that the 'centralization's' machine has not been so well oiled as to silence their voice." Here we have again this insinuation about a "machine," without any proof that such exists in fact. What a nice loophole that last sentence affords: Should Warwick and his allies fail to convince the majority of delegates to the convention, they can still lay the blame on the "machine." "New York, Philadelphia, and Boston are lined up solidly Who lined them up? We ask this with the 'decentralizers.' because the bulk of the membership in those named localities has only recently joined the I. W. W. They may be all readers of the "Social War" (though we very much doubt that), but few of them are readers of the I. W. W. papers. Few of them are acquainted with the history of the I. W. W., or with the acts and records of the officials they are asked and expected to condemn. Even Warwick admits they are in a passive state of mind, since "there has not been any considerable noise among the rank and file." Is it possible they are being imposed upon by Warwick and some few others we might name? Is it possible that the PERSONAL AMBITIONS of a few leaders in the East (who are always howling against "leaders") is leading them to take advantage of a sincere sentiment in the West, with a view to having the Western locals "pull their chestnuts out of the fire" for them? Anyway, it is worth while for the Western locals to bear this possibility in mind. Now, more than ever, should a SENSE OF RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE WELFARE OF THE I. W. W. actuate every live and sincere member. The enemy is busy trying to undermine our solidarity. Don't play into his hands, Make the Warwicks and others "come clean" with their proof, or stand branded as irresponsible "fishers in troubled waters."
